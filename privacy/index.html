Privacy Policy for MARKrosoft Applications

Effective date: August 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to desktop applications published by MARKrosoft unless a specific application provides a separate privacy notice.

Information collection

MARKrosoft applications do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information unless a specific application clearly states otherwise.

Omni Timer does not collect personal information, does not require a user account, and does not transmit user data to MARKrosoft.

Local application data

Applications may store settings, preferences, timers, selected alert sounds, and other configuration information locally on the user's device. This information remains on the device unless the user independently chooses to copy, back up, or synchronize it through another service.

Internet access

Omni Timer does not require an internet connection for its normal operation.

If a future MARKrosoft application connects to an online service, the purpose of that connection and any information transmitted will be disclosed within the application, its store listing, or an application specific privacy notice.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Omni Timer does not contain advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking services.

Device capabilities

Omni Timer does not access location data, contacts, camera data, microphone recordings, or personal account information.

The application may allow the user to select local sound files for use as timer alerts. These files are selected by the user and are not uploaded or transmitted to MARKrosoft.

Children's privacy

Omni Timer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when applications change or new applications are released. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
